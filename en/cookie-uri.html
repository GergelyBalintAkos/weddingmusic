Cookie Policy

Cookies are small files that a website can save in your browser, usually to remember preferences or measure traffic.

What We Use on This Site

The CLASSIS WeddingMusic website does not currently use analytics, marketing, or tracking cookies. We do not install Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or other similar tools.

External Fonts

To display text, we use fonts loaded from Google Fonts. This loading involves a connection to Google's servers, which may receive your device's IP address, but without installing dedicated cookies for this purpose. We mention this for the sake of transparency.

Controlling Cookies in Your Browser

Even though this site does not use its own tracking cookies, you can always check and control the cookies stored by any website in your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, etc.), including blocking or deleting them.

Future Changes

If we introduce analytics or marketing tools that use cookies in the future, we will update this policy and request your consent through a dedicated banner before installing any non-essential cookies.
