Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data we collect through the CLASSIS WeddingMusic website, for what purpose we use it, and what your rights are, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and applicable Romanian law.

Data Controller

S.C. ARTIST MANAGEMENT AGENCY S.R.L., headquartered in Cluj-Napoca, Str. Banul Udrea, No. 29, registered with the Trade Registry Office attached to the Cluj Court under no. J12/946/10.06.2010, Tax ID 27045543 (hereinafter referred to as "CLASSIS WeddingMusic" or "we").

1. What Data We Collect

Through the contact form, we collect the data you voluntarily provide: name, email address, phone number, event type, and the content of the message sent.

2. Purpose of Processing

We use this data exclusively to respond to your requests, prepare personalized offers, and organize the potential collaboration for your event.

3. Legal Basis

The processing is based on your consent, given by submitting the form, as well as on our legitimate interest in responding to requests received and, subsequently, on the performance of a potential service contract.

4. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell or rent your data to third parties for marketing purposes. To deliver messages from the contact form, we use the third-party service FormSubmit, which acts as a data processor and processes the data solely for the purpose of delivering the message to our email address.

5. Storage Period

The data is kept for as long as necessary to manage your request, and subsequently for as long as required by any legal obligations (e.g., contractual or tax records), after which it is deleted.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, object, and to data portability. You can exercise these rights by contacting us at info@weddingmusic.ro.

7. Right to Lodge a Complaint

If you believe that the processing of your data violates applicable law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP) – www.dataprotection.ro.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Any changes will be published on this page.
