Music for Christian weddings

Live music and Christian hymns arranged for our ensemble, adapted to the traditions of every Christian denomination.

At a Christian wedding, music accompanies the family's moments of thanksgiving, prayer and joy. Classis from Cluj-Napoca performs Christian hymns arranged for strings and piano live, alongside famous classical pieces, film music and well-known melodies, carefully chosen for the atmosphere of a Christian wedding.

Every piece is agreed in detail with the couple, so that the musical programme reflects the faith and wishes of your family.

Short programme

Full programme

throughout the event, in four or five sets, one for each course

Frequently asked questions

About Christian weddings

Which Christian hymns do you play?

We perform Christian hymns arranged for the ensemble, alongside classical pieces, film music and other well-known melodies. Every piece is agreed in detail with the couple, according to the wishes of the community and the family.

Do you also play in church?

Yes, we play at the church service wherever the tradition of the denomination allows instrumental music, as well as at the festive dinner, with a repertoire suited to every moment.

How long is the musical programme?

The short programme covers the guests' arrival, the couple's entrance and the cold starters. The full programme covers the whole event in four or five sets, one for each course: from the couple's entrance and the starters to the roast and the traditional march as the cake is brought in.

Which ensembles do you recommend for a Christian wedding?

A trio (two violins and piano), a piano quartet, a quintet or a six-piece chamber orchestra. The professional digital piano is included, and a grand piano can be provided on request.

Can you provide sound for the whole wedding?

Yes, on request and at extra cost: 2–6 speakers as needed, mixer and amplification, instrument microphones, voice microphones for announcements and music from a laptop during breaks. The ensemble's own sound during the performance is included in the quote.

Do you play outdoors?

Yes, provided the temperature is suitable for the instruments and there is a covered terrace or a tent in case the sun shines directly on the instruments or it rains.

How do we get a quote?

Send us the date, the venue, your denomination and the programme you would like through the contact form and we will reply with a personalised quote within 24 hours.
