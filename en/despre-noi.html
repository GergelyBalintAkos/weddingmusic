About us

About Us

With over 20 years of experience in event organization and musical production, we have built a career based on professionalism, creativity, and valuable collaborations. Every project is a new opportunity to create moments that remain in memory.

Our repertoire includes celebrated classical pieces, Viennese waltzes, tangos, salon music, café-concert, world hits, melodic jazz, film music, soundtracks, and modern hits.

To best meet your requirements and preferences, our repertoire is prepared for different musical formations, giving you the opportunity to choose the ensemble that best suits your event.

We offer the following artistic ensembles:

Trio

Three artists: two violins and piano

String Quartet

Four artists: two violins, viola, cello

Quartet with Piano

Four artists: two violins, viola and piano

Four artists: two violins, cello and piano

String Quintet

Five artists: two violins, viola, cello and double bass

Quintet with Piano

Five artists: two violins, viola, cello and piano

Mini Orchestra

Six artists: two violins, viola, cello, piano and double bass

Ambient Jazz Piano

Other artistic ensembles upon request
