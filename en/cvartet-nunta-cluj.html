Wedding music

Wedding string quartet and band in Cluj-Napoca

Live music performed by professional musicians, from the church ceremony to the first dance. Over 20 years of experience and more than 350 weddings.

Looking for a wedding band in Cluj that brings elegance to every moment? Classis is a wedding string quartet from Cluj-Napoca made up of professional musicians: violin, viola, cello, double bass and piano. We play only live music, at a volume that leaves room for conversation and emotion.

Choose the line-up that suits your wedding, from trio to chamber orchestra, and a repertoire tailored to every moment: classical music, waltzes, tango, film music and modern hits arranged for strings.

Wedding moments

Live music for every moment

Church ceremony

Solemn music in church, from the bride's entrance to the couple's exit. For evangelical weddings we have a dedicated repertoire of Christian hymns.

Civil ceremony

A few well-chosen pieces turn the civil ceremony into a memorable moment, at the town hall or outdoors.

Cocktail and guest reception

The quartet creates a refined atmosphere as guests arrive, with ambient music, waltzes and melodic jazz.

Dinner and first dance

Live music during dinner and an unforgettable first dance, played live to the song of your choice.

Frequently asked questions

About wedding music

Why choose a string quartet for your wedding?

A string quartet brings the elegance of live music: it sounds beautiful in church, at the civil ceremony and at the cocktail, without excessive volume, and its repertoire ranges from classical music to modern pieces.

Can you play the song we choose for our first dance?

Yes. The repertoire can be personalised on request, so your first dance can be played live to the song of your choice.

Do you also play in church?

Yes, we play at church ceremonies wherever the tradition of the denomination allows instrumental music, with a repertoire of Christian hymns adapted to each denomination.

Which cities do you play weddings in?

Cluj-Napoca, Turda, Dej, Gherla, Zalău, Bistrița, Baia Mare, Alba Iulia, Aiud, Târgu Mureș and Bucharest, as well as other places around the country.

How much does a wedding band cost?

The price depends on the ensemble, the length of the programme and the wedding venue. Send us the date, the venue and the moments you have in mind through the contact form and we will reply with a personalised quote within 24 hours.

Do you bring sound equipment?

Yes. We bring a Dynacord mixer-amplifier, DPA 4099 microphones for the string instruments and an electric piano. On request we also provide full sound for the wedding: 2–6 speakers as needed, voice microphones for announcements and music from a laptop during breaks. See our equipment.
