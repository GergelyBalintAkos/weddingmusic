Repertoire

Our musical ensembles have a varied repertoire, suitable for ceremonies, receptions, private events, concerts and cultural events. The program can be adapted according to the specifics of the event and the preferences of the organizers.

The repertoire includes:

Classical & Viennese
  • classical music by great composers;
  • elegant, charming Viennese waltzes.
Café-concert & jazz
  • café-concert style tangos;
  • international hits and evergreen melodies;
  • melodic jazz and salon music.
Film & contemporary
  • soundtracks and famous film themes;
  • pop and contemporary pieces, arranged for string ensembles.
Ambient
  • ambient music, ideal for creating a refined and relaxing atmosphere.
Christian hymns
  • dedicated to Protestant ceremonies and weddings.

Upon request, the repertoire can be customized to best match the wishes and theme of each event.

To view the full list of pieces in our repertoire, please contact us.
