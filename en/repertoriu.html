Repertoire

Our ensembles perform a wide range of repertoire, carefully selected to suit wedding ceremonies, receptions, corporate events, private celebrations, concerts, and cultural occasions. Every performance can be specially tailored to suit the style of your event and personal preferences.

Our repertoire includes:

Classical & Viennese
  • Classical masterpieces by renowned composers;
  • Elegant and timeless Viennese waltzes.
Café Concert & Jazz
  • Traditional café concert music and tangos;
  • International classics and evergreen favourites;
  • Melodic jazz and salon music.
Film & Contemporary
  • Iconic film scores and memorable movie themes;
  • Contemporary and pop arrangements for string ensembles.
Background Music
  • Elegant background music, perfect for creating a refined and relaxing atmosphere.
Christian Hymns
  • Sacred music for Protestant wedding ceremonies and religious services.

Our repertoire can always be customized to perfectly reflect the style, theme, and atmosphere of your event.

To receive our complete repertoire list, please don't hesitate to contact us.
