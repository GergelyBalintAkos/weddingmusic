General Conditions

for the performance of artistic interpretations

For our musical ensembles' performance to take place under the best conditions, and for the instruments to be properly protected, please take the following recommendations into account.

Outdoor Performances

String instruments are sensitive to weather conditions, which is why outdoor performances can only take place if adequate conditions are met.

  • Artists must not be exposed to direct sunlight during the performance.
  • In case of rain, drizzle or excessive humidity, the performance will be moved indoors or to a fully covered area (terrace, gazebo, pavilion, tent, etc.).
  • The performance area must be sheltered from strong wind, which can affect both the instruments and the sheet music.
  • The temperature must be comfortable for both artists and instruments. In extreme heat or cold conditions, the ensemble reserves the right to request that the performance be moved to a suitable space.
  • The organizer is asked to prepare a backup option (Plan B) in advance for situations where the weather does not allow the event to take place outdoors.

Indoor Performances

The following are required for the optimal performance of the artistic program:

  • sufficient space for the ensemble and music stands;
  • a flat, stable and clean surface;
  • adequate lighting, so that the sheet music can be read comfortably;
  • easy access to the performance location;
  • a nearby power source, when sound amplification or additional lighting is required.

Performance Space

We recommend:

  • an area of approximately 6–8 m² for a trio/quartet;
  • one chair prepared for each artist (chairs without armrests);
  • free space around the ensemble, so that bow movements are not obstructed by furniture or guests;
  • placing the ensemble in a visible area that does not obstruct the flow of guests.

Instrument Protection

The instruments used are of great value and require special conditions of use. For this reason, the ensembles cannot perform in situations where the instruments are exposed to:

  • rain, water splashes or spraying installations;
  • strong sun and excessive heat;
  • high humidity;
  • strong wind;
  • artificial smoke, dry ice, special effects or confetti jets placed in the immediate vicinity;
  • risk of accidental impact from guests or staff.

If these conditions occur during the event, the ensemble may temporarily interrupt the performance until the situation is resolved.

Access and Organization

For the smooth running of the event, we recommend:

  • the ensemble's access to the venue at least 30 minutes before the program starts;
  • having a contact person from the organizer or the venue;
  • prior notice of any changes to the program or location;
  • if the ensemble needs to move to another space during the event, it is recommended that the new location be prepared before the move.

Sound Amplification

String ensembles typically perform acoustically. For events held in very large spaces or outdoors, the organizer may provide a sound system, if deemed necessary and arranged in advance.

Breaks

For longer artistic programs, the musicians will have short breaks between performance segments, according to the schedule agreed with the organizer.

Final Recommendations

We want every event to enjoy an elegant, top-quality musical experience. Respecting these conditions contributes to both the smooth running of the artistic program and the protection of the instruments and the comfort of the musicians.

Thank you for your understanding and cooperation!
