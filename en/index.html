About Classis

Live music performed with refinement

Classis brings together professional musicians from Cluj-Napoca, with over 20 years of experience in concerts, music productions and private events. Violin, viola, cello, double bass and piano create an elegant atmosphere, suited to a gala or a corporate reception as much as to the most personal moments of your life.

Our live music ensembles adapt to every event: trio, string quartet, piano quartet, quintet or chamber orchestra. The repertoire includes classical music, Viennese waltzes, tango, melodic jazz, film music and contemporary pieces arranged for strings.

Frequently asked questions

Useful information

Which live music ensembles do you offer?

Trio (two violins and piano), string quartet, piano quartet, string quintet, piano quintet, a six-piece chamber orchestra and ambient piano jazz. Other line-ups are available on request.

What kinds of events do you play at?

Corporate events, galas, conferences, concerts, receptions and private events such as anniversaries, civil ceremonies and weddings.

What repertoire do you play?

Classical music, Viennese waltzes, tango, salon and café-concert music, melodic jazz, film music and contemporary pieces arranged for strings. For church ceremonies we also have a repertoire of Christian hymns. See the repertoire.

Which cities do you perform in?

Cluj-Napoca, Turda, Dej, Gherla, Zalău, Bistrița, Baia Mare, Alba Iulia, Aiud, Târgu Mureș and Bucharest, as well as other cities around the country.

How do I get a quote?

The price depends on the ensemble, the length of the programme and the venue. Send us the details through the contact form and we will reply with a personalised quote within 24 hours.

Do you bring sound equipment?

Yes. We bring a Dynacord mixer-amplifier, DPA 4099 microphones for the string instruments and an electric piano. RCF speakers can be requested separately. See our equipment.
